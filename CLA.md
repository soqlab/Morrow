This agreement has two parts. Part A is for an individual contributing on their own behalf. Part B
is for a company or other organization that lets its employees or contractors contribute. Both share
the definitions and the terms in sections 1 to 8.

## How you agree

- **Individuals (Part A):** tick "I agree to CLA.md" in the pull request template when you open a
  pull request. Ticking the box, with the GitHub account that opens the pull request, is your
  agreement to Part A for that contribution and every later one you submit.
- **Organizations (Part B):** a person with authority to bind the organization emails
  contact@soqlab.com with the organization's legal name and address, the statement "We agree to Part
  B of the Morrow Contributor Licence Agreement, version 1.0", and the GitHub accounts of the people
  authorised to contribute on its behalf (Schedule A). The organization tells SOQLAB when that list
  changes. Each of those people also ticks the box in their pull requests.

If you contribute as part of your job, your employer may own what you write. In that case either
your employer agrees to Part B, or you get your employer's permission (or a written waiver of its
rights) before you agree to Part A. If you are unsure, ask before contributing.
