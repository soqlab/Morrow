How recovery works

A recovery code works once and lets you create a new passkey. No code left? An admin of your organization can make you a recovery link in Morrow and give it to you; it works once and expires after 30 minutes. Creating a new passkey with a code or a link signs you out on every other device. A setup link from an admin works once and creates a new account's first passkey. Servers that run their own Morrow may also offer recovery by email. If your organization signs in through single sign-on, recover your account with that provider instead.
