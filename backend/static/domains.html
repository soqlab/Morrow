Why this is needed

Morrow connects to the servers you scan. So that nobody can point it at systems they don't run, a hosted account can only scan domains it has verified. Verifying example.com lets you scan example.com and any name under it, such as vpn.example.com. IP addresses can't be verified, so scan by host name. The record can stay in place; removing it doesn't remove the domain here.
