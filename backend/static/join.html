How joining works

An admin of the organization makes a single-use invite link in Morrow and gives it to you however they like: Morrow sends nothing. You open it and create a passkey on this device: there is no password and no email. New members start as viewers, and an admin of the organization can change that. Already have an account? Sign in instead. Starting your own organization? Create an account.
