What you get

Your own organization in Morrow, with you as its admin. Watch any domain and hear when its answer changes. Claim the domains you own with a DNS record, then scan them and everything under them. Morrow scans only domains your organization has proved it controls; the public check on any domain's page works as before.

You sign in with a passkey: your fingerprint, face, device PIN or a security key. After the passkey is made you get ten recovery codes, shown once: save them, because they're how you get back in if you lose the passkey. Morrow never emails you. Already have an account? Sign in.
