CUSTOMER CONTROL / AI TRANSPARENCY

Your data is not
an open invitation.

AI starts off. You decide the destination, workflows and data it may receive. Inspect the boundary, export the evidence, or stop new processing.

Inspect your controls

What you control

Organization administrators approve a versioned policy: AI off, customer-hosted processing, or an explicitly approved external provider. The endpoint and model are pinned to that approval. The application never silently falls back to another provider.

The default data permission allows only bounded numeric facts and known cryptographic algorithm/TLS values. Arbitrary text, identifiers and unknown fields are withheld. Broader free-text sharing requires explicit approval and is not complete data-loss prevention.

Filter before uploading

Download the customer filtering kit from Data Control. It reads one JSON export you select, removes unsupported data locally, and shows the exact minimized payload without networking. Sending requires approval of a digest binding that payload, destination and policy version.

A browser preview has already reached the platform. Use the offline kit when raw data must not leave your environment. Other platform upload and scan APIs are separate; this kit does not automatically intercept them.

AI is an analyst, not an administrator

The conversational AI has one read-only organization-summary tool. It cannot autonomously browse your repositories, scan arbitrary targets, open or merge pull requests, or change your infrastructure. Explicit console actions retain their own access checks. Non-AI scanning remains available with AI off.

Human access you can inspect

Vendor support sessions require a customer-enabled setting, defaulting to denied when no setting exists. Your admin can inspect session request history and revoke access. Platform owners cannot override that decision through the support-session API. This setting does not restrict direct database or infrastructure administrators; address those privileges in your deployment and contract.

Evidence with honest limits

Gateway receipts sign policy changes, dispatch authorization and observed response outcomes without retaining prompt or response bodies in the receipts. Export and independently verify them against a pinned Ed25519 key. They authenticate gateway records, not the absence of other traffic, provider deletion or training behavior. Ed25519 receipts are not zero-knowledge proofs or post-quantum signatures.

Stop AI blocks new and queued requests, but cannot recall an already-dispatching or transmitted request. Retention controls apply to these receipts, not provider systems, backups or other platform journals.

What still needs independent validation

Customer-hosted processing requires deployment in your environment and an approved model endpoint; its location is operator-declared, not remotely attested. Complete outbound restrictions require a deployment firewall. Provider retention, training settings, regions and contractual commitments must be verified for your actual account. No certification or blanket guarantee is implied.
